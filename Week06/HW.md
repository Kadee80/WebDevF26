Presenting is what marks your midterm **on time**. Your code is graded later,
which means the week after you present is still real build time.

## This week

- [ ] **Act on the feedback you got.** If we agreed to cut a page or simplify
      the navigation, do that first, and update your wireframe to match.

- [ ] In your class repo, make a `Midterm` folder. Inside it:
  - `index.html` — your home page, marked up in real, semantic HTML
  - `css/style.css` — linked and doing something, even if it is rough
  - `img/` — your images, sized sensibly before you put them in the page
  - `README.md` — a short write-up of the concept, plus your wireframe or
    sitemap (a photo of a hand-drawn one is fine)

- [ ] **Structure it, do not decorate it.** Header, main content, footer, real
      headings, real lists. Layout before decoration — you have two more weeks
      to make it pretty.

- [ ] **At least one media query doing real work.** Use Thursday's lesson on
      your own page, not just on the demo. If you only write one, make it
      mobile: `(max-width: 768px)`.

- [ ] Commit and push. Small commits with real messages, not one giant
      `git commit -m "stuff"` at midnight.
