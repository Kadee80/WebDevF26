# HOMEWORK: GET READY TO PRESENT — WEEK 08

You present next week. **Tuesday is volunteers only, for a bonus 5 points;
Thursday is everybody.** Email me if you want the Tuesday slot — my address is
in the [syllabus](../SYLLABUS.md).

Your *code* is graded at the end of Week 09, so presenting is about showing
real progress, not a finished product.

## Have this ready

- [ ] **A demo of the site** and the progress you have made. Run it from your
      GitHub Pages link if you have one — and after Thursday, you have no
      excuse not to.
- [ ] **One snippet of code you figured out** — a tutorial you adapted, or
      responsive behaviour you worked out. Be ready to explain how it works. If
      you cannot explain it, do not put it in the presentation.
- [ ] **At least one media query doing real work.** I will ask you to resize
      your window during the demo, so test it first. If you only write one,
      make it mobile: `(max-width: 768px)`.
- [ ] **Next steps** — what you would do with more time, or what carries into
      your final project.
- [ ] **Everything in your repo**: project code, documentation, and a copy of
      your slides if you use them.

Five minutes each. Practice it once out loud beforehand; it is always longer
than you think.

## This week's work

- [ ] **Host it.** Get the midterm onto **GitHub Pages** and put the live link
      in your `Midterm/README.md` and in your repo's main `README.md`. You did
      this with the contact form on Thursday; the second time takes five
      minutes. A site nobody can visit is not finished.
- [ ] **Pull your colours into CSS variables.** Find the hex codes you have
      repeated, name them at `:root`, and swap them for `var()`. Cheapest
      possible improvement to a stylesheet you are still changing your mind
      about.
- [ ] **Add the contact form** to your site — a contact page, or a section on
      the home page. Your own colours, which now means redefining variables
      rather than rewriting rules.
- [ ] **One thing that moves.** A hover transform on your gallery, a modal, a
      button that responds. Something from Tuesday, doing a real job.
- [ ] Open the site on your **actual phone**, not a narrow browser window.
      Those are not the same test.

## Before Tuesday

Make sure **Adobe Photoshop** is installed — Week 08 opens with image sizing
and optimization, and it is much easier to follow along than to watch.

## If something has gone wrong

If you cannot present in Week 08, email me **before** the fact and we will work
it out. After the fact is a different conversation.
