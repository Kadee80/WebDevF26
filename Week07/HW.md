## Your presentation — five minutes

Whether you go Tuesday or Thursday, it is the same five minutes. Your *code* is
graded at the end of Week 09, so presenting is about showing real progress, not
a finished product.

- [ ] **A demo of the site** and the progress you have made. Run it locally if
      you are not hosted yet — hosting is Tuesday's lesson, so nobody is
      penalised for not having a link on the day.
- [ ] **One snippet of code you figured out** — a tutorial you adapted, or
      responsive behaviour you worked out. Be ready to explain how it works. If
      you cannot explain it, do not put it in the presentation.
- [ ] **Your media query**, demonstrated live by resizing.
- [ ] **Next steps** — what you would do with more time, or what carries into
      your final project.
- [ ] **Everything in your repo**: project code, documentation, and a copy of
      your slides if you use them.

Practise it once out loud beforehand; it is always longer than you think. A
slide deck is strongly recommended.
