Buttermilk Pancakes

Light, fluffy pancakes that take about twenty minutes start to finish. Makes roughly eight.

The trick is not overmixing the batter. A few lumps are what you want. If you beat it smooth you will get pancakes with the texture of a hockey puck, and you cannot un-mix a batter, so stop early.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup melted butter
  • 2 tbsp whole milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup all purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter and let it cool slightly.
  2. Beat together the milk, butter and egg.
  3. Add the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt.
  4. Mix until just combined. Lumps are fine.
  5. Drop by the tablespoon into a hot buttered pan.
  6. Flip when the top is full of little holes.

Notes

Substitutions

No buttermilk? Add a tablespoon of lemon juice to whole milk and wait five minutes.

Leftovers

Freezing

Cool completely, then freeze flat on a sheet pan before bagging.

Reheating

Toaster

One cycle on medium. Best texture.

Microwave

Twenty seconds. Faster, sadder.
