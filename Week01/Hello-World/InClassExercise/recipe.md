# Buttermilk Pancakes

![A tall stack of buttermilk pancakes with butter melting down the side](img/pancakes.jpg)

Light, fluffy, and about twenty minutes start to finish. Makes roughly **twelve
four-inch pancakes**, which is enough for four people or two very committed
ones.

The trick is _not_ overmixing the batter. Lumps are correct. If you beat it
smooth you get pancakes with the texture of a hockey puck, and you cannot
un-mix a batter.

## Ingredients

### Dry

- 1½ cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt

### Wet

- 1½ cups buttermilk
- 1 large egg
- 3 tablespoons butter, melted and cooled slightly
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional, but do it)

You will also need a little more butter for the pan.

## Instructions

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a
   large bowl.
2. In a second bowl, beat the egg, then whisk in the buttermilk, melted butter,
   and vanilla.
3. Pour the wet into the dry. Stir until _just_ combined — about ten strokes.
   Stop while it still looks lumpy.
4. Let the batter rest for 10 minutes. Do not skip this. It is what makes them
   fluffy.
5. Heat a skillet over medium and butter it lightly.
6. Pour about ¼ cup of batter per pancake. Leave room; they spread.
7. Flip when the top is covered in little holes and the edges look dry, about
   2 minutes.
8. Cook another 1–2 minutes on the second side, until golden.
9. Serve immediately, or hold them in a 200°F oven while you cook the rest.

## Notes

### No buttermilk?

Add 1½ tablespoons of lemon juice or white vinegar to 1½ cups of whole milk and
let it sit for five minutes. It will look slightly curdled. That is correct.

### Leftovers

Cool completely, then freeze flat on a sheet pan before bagging them, so they
do not fuse into one brick. Reheat straight from frozen:

- **Toaster** - one cycle on medium. Best texture.
- **Microwave** - twenty seconds. Faster, sadder.
