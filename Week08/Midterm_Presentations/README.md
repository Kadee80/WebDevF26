# Midterm Presentations

Everybody presents today. If you volunteered and already went on Tuesday, you
still come to class — you are part of the audience, and audience is graded.

Presentation order is shuffled at the start of class so nobody has to sit and
dread being last.

## When it is your turn

Share your screen and walk us through:

- **A demo of the site** and the progress you have made.
- **One snippet of code you figured out** — a tutorial you adapted, or some
  responsive behaviour you worked out. Be ready to explain how it works. If you
  cannot explain it, do not put it in the presentation.
- **Your responsive behaviour.** I will ask you to resize your window, so have
  at least one media query doing real work. If you only write one, make it
  mobile: `(max-width: 768px)`.
- **Next steps** — what you would do with more time, or what you plan to carry
  into your final project.

Five minutes each. Practice it once out loud beforehand; it is always longer
than you think.

## When it is not your turn

**Laptops closed.** Give your classmates the same attention you want when you
are up there. Participation is 25% of your grade and this is one of the days it
is most visible.

You will also see solutions to problems you are stuck on. Every semester
somebody solves in week 8 the exact thing somebody else is fighting with.

## What is being graded today

Presenting is what marks your midterm **on time**. Your *code* is not graded
today — see [HW.md](../HW.md) for the full timeline.
