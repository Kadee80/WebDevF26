## Class notes

- **Tuesday:** [Contact Form, CSS Variables & GitHub Pages](../Week07/Week7_2/CSS_Vars_Contact_Form/README.md) — a form that really sends, and your site on the internet. *(The notes live in the Week 07 folder, where they were written — the class moved, the file did not.)*
- **Thursday:** [Midterm Presentations](Midterm_Presentations/README.md)

**Tuesday is the one to be in the room for.** By the end of it your site is at a
real URL, and a site nobody can visit is not finished.

Nothing to install. Bring a laptop you can log into GitHub on — anybody who
cannot get in loses the last half hour of the lesson.

## Presenting

Five minutes each. Tuesday's slots are volunteers only, worth a bonus 5 points —
email me to claim one, first come first served. Thursday is everybody else, in
an order shuffled at the start of class.

If you present Tuesday you still come on Thursday. Audience is graded;
participation is 25%.

Your *code* is graded at the end of Week 09, so presenting is about showing real
progress, not a finished product. Full checklist in
[Week07/HW.md](../Week07/HW.md).
